# Call 04: Carrier technical support, 38AU with equalized pressures

TECH: I'm on a Carrier 38AU. Compressor's drawing amps, but suction and discharge are sitting equal and it's not cooling. There's a Copeland ASTP label on the compressor. Is the compressor dead?
REP: Probably not. That's Advanced Scroll Temperature Protection. There's a bimetal disk inside that opens at about three hundred degrees internal temperature and unloads the scrolls. So it runs, but it doesn't pump.
TECH: How do I get it back?
REP: Stop the compressor and let it cool. ASTP resets on its own before the motor protector does, and that can take up to two hours. There's a cool-down chart in the installation instructions, and the time goes up with how long it ran unloaded.
TECH: Okay. Then I just restart it?
REP: Don't just restart it. Something drove the discharge temperature that high. Check your superheat and the charge, look for a restriction, and make sure the condenser fans actually run.
TECH: Sure.
REP: And after you restart, clamp a thermocouple on the discharge line about six inches from the compressor. If it climbs above roughly two hundred twenty-five degrees, shut it back down and keep looking.
TECH: Six inches out, two twenty-five. Got it.
