# Call 01: Copeland application engineering, CoreSense red 3 flashes

TECH: Hi, I'm on a residential job, ZPS forty-something K5 UltraTech in a two-stage condenser, it's got the CoreSense module on it. Right now the red LED is flashing three times. Last two visits somebody noted yellow three.
REP: Okay. Yellow three is pressure switch cycling. The module sees the compressor run anywhere from about twelve seconds to fifteen minutes and then drop out for thirty-five seconds up to seven minutes. That's the high or low pressure switch opening.
TECH: And red three?
REP: Red three means it's gone to lockout on that same code. It locks the compressor out after four consecutive Code 3 events or ten total.
TECH: Got it. So I just cycle power to the module to clear it?
REP: Cycling power to the module clears it, yes, but only once you've fixed the cause. And before you cycle it, take a photo of the LED or write down the flash history, because once you reset it that history is the only evidence you've got.
TECH: Makes sense. The switch itself could be bad though, right?
REP: It could, but it's usually not the switch. Put your gauges on, get it running, and compare condensing saturation temperature to outdoor ambient. On a standard-efficiency condenser, if that split is much above about thirty degrees, you're looking at a dirty coil or an airflow problem, not a bad pressure switch.
TECH: Thirty degrees over ambient. Okay, the coil does look pretty matted from the cottonwood.
REP: There you go. Clean it, verify the fan, then reset the module and watch a couple of cycles.
TECH: Will do. Thanks.
