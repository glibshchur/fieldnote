# Call 03: Mitsubishi support, PUZ-A36NHA3 U1 lockout

TECH: I've got a PUZ-A36NHA3 that keeps locking out on U1 in cooling. Only happens in the afternoon when it's hot. Indoor filter's clean, I checked the stop valves, they're full open.
REP: U1 is abnormal high pressure. The high-pressure switch, 63H, opened while the compressor was running. That switch trips at 4.15 megapascal, 602 psig.
TECH: So what's on the list outdoors?
REP: Outdoor fan motor locked or malfunctioning, a dirty outdoor heat exchanger, the outdoor unit short cycling on air, and then the parts: outdoor thermistor, the 63H connector, or the controller board.
TECH: The fan's spinning though. I watched it.
REP: Spinning isn't the same as moving air. Clamp the outdoor fan motor lead while it's running. Then with power off, grab the blade and check it by hand. If the motor turns but the blade slips on the shaft, or the amp draw is way below normal, the blade or the hub is loose and it's not moving enough air, even though it looks like it's running.
TECH: Huh. That would explain why it only trips when it's hot.
REP: Exactly. Check that before you go after the board.
