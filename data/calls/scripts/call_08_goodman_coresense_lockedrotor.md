# Call 08: Goodman technical support, DSZ CoreSense red LOCK 4 flashes

TECH: Goodman DSZ heat pump with the CoreSense module. Red LOCK light flashing four times, yellow's off. Homeowner says it tried to start a bunch of times overnight. Can I just reset the module?
REP: Red four with yellow off is locked rotor. The module locks the compressor out after ten consecutive locked-rotor events, so it's been trying a while.
TECH: What's the usual cause?
REP: Run capacitor failed, low line voltage, and if the voltage at the disconnect is low you check wiring or call the utility. Then excessive liquid refrigerant in the compressor, or seized bearings, and for that you'd check the oil level.
TECH: Okay, so reset and try it?
REP: One try, and be ready. Put a clamp meter on the compressor common lead before you reset. If the current stays at locked-rotor level for more than about three seconds, kill the power right away. Don't let the module keep retrying. Then go straight to the capacitor and voltage checks.
TECH: Three seconds. Got it.
