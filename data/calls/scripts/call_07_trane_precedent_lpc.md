# Call 07: Trane support, Precedent YHJ circuit 1 low pressure lockout

TECH: Trane Precedent YHJ, Symbio 700. Circuit one compressor is locked out on a low pressure diagnostic. Cool spring morning, we just changed filters. It won't restart on its own. Why'd it lock out instead of just waiting out the delay?
REP: When the low pressure input opens for one continuous second while the compressor's running, the compressor shuts off and can't restart for at least three minutes. If it opens four times in a row during the first three minutes of operation, that circuit locks out and needs a manual reset.
TECH: So I reset it.
REP: Not yet. Put gauges on it first, and run the supply fan by itself for about fifteen minutes so any frost on the evaporator clears. Check the belt, check the filters you just put in are the right ones, and make sure the outdoor air dampers aren't wide open to cold air.
TECH: Okay.
REP: Then reset and watch suction pressure through the first three minutes. If it still dips toward the cut-out, stop resetting it and go look for low charge or a restriction. Resetting it over and over just repeats the lockout.
