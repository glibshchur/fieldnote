# Call 06: Bryant technical support, 569J center fans not running

TECH: I'm on a Bryant 569J, low ambient package. It's about fifty-eight out. The two outer condenser fans are running but the center ones, OFM2 and OFM4, are dead. I'm about to order motors.
REP: Hold off. On that unit the center fans are switched by the low ambient temperature switch, the LAS, through the low ambient relay, LAR. The LAS opens at fifty-five and closes at sixty, plus or minus about two degrees. At fifty-eight those fans can legitimately be off.
TECH: Oh. Okay.
REP: If you want to prove the motors, briefly jumper the LAS contacts and let them run, maybe two minutes. If they run, the motors are fine.
TECH: And if the switching seems off?
REP: Look at where the LAS sensor is sitting. If it's in direct sun or in the coil discharge air, it'll read wrong and the switching temperature will look off.
TECH: It's actually right in the discharge path. Someone zip-tied it there.
REP: That'll do it. Move it back.
